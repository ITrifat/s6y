01 / ABOUT THE ARCHITECT

Most businesses don't have an AI problem.
They have a workflow problem.

I focus on the layer between business strategy and technical execution: understanding how work actually moves, finding the friction, then designing intelligent systems around it.

I approach automation from the process first — mapping operations, defining decisions and exceptions, designing the workflow, then deciding where AI agents, integrations and human approvals create the most leverage.

The goal is not to add AI for the sake of AI. The goal is to build reliable, measurable and scalable operations.
